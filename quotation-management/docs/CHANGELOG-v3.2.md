# NexziByte Quotation Management — v3.2 UI and quotation updates

- Fixed the PDF terms-and-conditions text field sizing and repositioned payment, delivery, validity, signature, and footer sections to prevent the common text-overlap seen in the supplied quotation.
- Added a per-quotation accent palette for quotation outlines and accent rules. The company logo, logo colours, and overall document structure are unchanged.
- Replaced starter client-product placeholders with a hardware/software catalogue: Dell business laptop, keyboard, desktop CPU, monitor, SSD, RAM, USB-C dock, UPS, and software licences.
- Removed legacy client-specific templates from the selectable template register and product API. The standard NexziByte template is used for new installs.
- Consolidated the sidebar quotation entry into “Add quotation”. The quotation pages now have a top tab bar for “Quotation register” and “New quotation”. The lighter SaaS interface is scoped to quotation pages; the rest of the application shell and logo are unchanged.

## Important pricing note
The new catalogue includes illustrative starter prices in INR so the products can be demonstrated. An administrator should confirm vendor, configuration, licence term, and prices before issuing a customer quotation.
