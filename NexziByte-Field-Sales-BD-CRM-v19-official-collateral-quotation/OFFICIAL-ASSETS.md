# Official client-facing assets

The following files were supplied for this CRM release and are used without redesigning their content:

- `backend/storage/collateral/NexziByte-Sales-Brochure.pdf`
- `backend/storage/collateral/NexziByte-Company-Profile.pdf`
- `backend/storage/collateral/Tejaswini-Gangula-Visiting-Card.png`

Generated quotation PDFs use the supplied commercial structure and the authorized signature embedded in the supplied quotation. Do not replace the signature or stamp without written authorization from NexziByte management.
