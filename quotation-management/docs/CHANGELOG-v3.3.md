- Increased quotation supplier/client card height so the email field and bottom border no longer collide.
- Shifted subject and line-item sections down to respect the taller client card.

- Increased the terms-and-conditions editing area and adjusted payment/delivery/validity spacing.
- Improved quotation register text contrast, filter readability, and action button visibility.
- Made the quotation-only register/new-quotation tab bar sticky and more prominent.
- Company logo assets and their source colours were not changed.
